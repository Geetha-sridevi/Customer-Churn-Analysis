# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project name
Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## Business objective
Summarize customer churn and allow stakeholders to explore churn patterns by contract, tenure, payment method, and monthly charges.

## Stakeholders
Business Analyst, Customer Retention Team, Reporting/Data Team.

## Business questions
1. How many customers churned in the dataset?
2. How does churn vary by contract type?
3. How do average monthly charges compare between churned and retained customers?
4. How does average tenure compare by churn status?

## Scope
- Analyze the supplied practice dataset.
- Create SQL summaries, Excel PivotTables, and a Power BI dashboard.
- Document requirements and test expected behavior.

## Out of scope
- Predictive machine learning.
- Production deployment.
- Real customer outreach or retention campaigns.

## Assumptions and limitations
The dataset contains 30 synthetic rows. Results are illustrative only and must be validated against larger, authorized data before business use.
